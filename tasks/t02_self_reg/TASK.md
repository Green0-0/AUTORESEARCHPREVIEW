Self-Regularizing Network (shortened proxy version, CIFAR-100).

We begin with one dataset: CIFAR-100. We take a fixed 500-image subset of the CIFAR-100 training set, with 5 images per class (chosen once with a fixed seed and provided). For the purposes of training, only these 500 images and their labels may be used: no other CIFAR images, no external data, no pretrained weights or externally obtained models. The goal is to find novel and effective models, trained from scratch on these 500 images, that achieve high top-5 accuracy on the full 10,000-image CIFAR-100 test set.

However, training-only regularization is banned. The manner in which the model processes data during training must be exactly the same as the way the model processes data during test time. Techniques such as dropout, stochastic depth, BatchNorm with batch statistics, random crops or flips, cutout, mixup or input noise are therefore allowed only if they are part of the model's forward pass and so are applied at test time too, in exactly the same way and with exactly the same parameters as during training. Concretely: the model receives raw images and does all of its own input processing (including any augmentation) inside its forward pass; the training loss must be computed on the model's output for raw training images; the model must behave identically in training and evaluation mode; and each example's output must not depend on the other examples in the batch. Any randomness inside the forward pass is also used at test time, where the evaluator seeds the random number generator once and makes a single forward pass per test batch (so, for example, averaging over several random views must be built into the forward pass itself). Choices that do not change how the model processes data, such as the architecture, initialization, optimizer, learning-rate schedule, weight decay, the loss function or self-distillation from the model's own outputs on raw images, are free.

Proxy budget: training must finish within 15 minutes on one NVIDIA A5000 GPU. Evaluation is done separately and does not count toward the budget.
