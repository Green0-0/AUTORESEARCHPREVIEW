Tokenizer Transfer (shortened proxy version, GPT-2 small to Japanese).

Take the pretrained GPT-2 small checkpoint (124M parameters, English, 50,257-token byte-level BPE vocabulary) and a fixed 200,000-row subset of Japanese Wikipedia (provided, both as raw text and pre-tokenized). The tokenizer is replaced by a new 32,000-token byte-level BPE tokenizer that was trained only on this Japanese subset. The new tokenizer is fixed and provided, and may not be changed: all perplexities are measured under it. The goal is to adapt GPT-2 small to the new tokenizer so that it achieves minimum per-token perplexity on a held-out 5,000-row Japanese Wikipedia set (scored with a fixed context of 1,024 tokens and a stride of 512), while keeping adaptation quick.

The model architecture must stay GPT-2 small: the 12 transformer blocks, position embeddings and final layer norm keep their shapes, and only the vocabulary-dependent token embedding and LM head change (to 32,000 rows; tying them is optional). Everything else is free: how the new embeddings and LM head are initialized (for example from the GPT-2 embeddings of related old tokens), vocabulary-mapping tricks, which parameters are trained when, the optimizer and schedule, the loss, and using the original GPT-2 (with its own tokenizer) as a teacher. Only the provided GPT-2 small checkpoint, the provided Japanese training subset and the provided tokenizers may be used: no other data, no other pretrained models or embeddings, and no network access.

Proxy budget: adaptation, including everything the solution does (initialization, any preprocessing, all training), must finish within 15 minutes on one NVIDIA A5000 GPU. Evaluation is done separately and does not count toward the budget.
