Few-Shot Segmentation (shortened proxy version, Pascal VOC 2012 with 5 images per class).

Begin with the Pascal VOC 2012 semantic segmentation task (21 classes: background plus 20 object classes). For each of the 20 object classes, only 5 fully annotated training images are provided (100 images in total, drawn once with a fixed seed from the VOC 2012 train split; every image keeps its full 21-class annotation). Train a model that maximizes mean IoU over the 21 classes on the full VOC 2012 validation set (1,449 images). All images are pre-resized to a fixed resolution (longer side 320 pixels, padded to 320x320), and evaluation is done at that resolution with a fixed protocol.

One fixed, well-pretrained ImageNet model is provided: a torchvision ResNet-50 with its ImageNet-1k weights. Using this model in any way is allowed (as a frozen or fine-tuned backbone, with dilation, as a teacher, and so on), and the decoder, losses, augmentation and training procedure are free. Pretraining on any segmentation or detection dataset is banned, and no other pretrained weights may be used (no DeepLab/FCN COCO or VOC weights, no SAM, CLIP, DINO or similar). No other data may be used: not the remaining VOC images, not SBD or COCO, and not ImageNet itself, which is not provided in this shortened version. There is no network access.

Proxy budget: training, including everything the solution does (model construction, any preprocessing, all training steps), must finish within 15 minutes on one NVIDIA A5000 GPU. Evaluation is done separately and does not count toward the budget.
